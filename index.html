What is Somonitor?

Somonitor is an always-on social and brand listening app. It tracks share of voice, decodes the tones, emotions and tensions in real conversations, and raises early warnings when a topic starts to turn. Its listening layer runs on SOMIN, an AI audience-research platform and Somonitor's technology partner, so every conclusion links back to the posts that evidence it.

The problem

Most listening tools count mentions. Few tell you what people mean.

Marketing teams are asked to measure audiences across platforms that do not talk to each other, to spot a crisis before it trends, and to take niche communities seriously before a competitor does. Most dashboards answer with volume charts and a sentiment line that flattens sarcasm, worry and affection into the same grey.

At the same time, audiences have grown wary of brands that seem to know them too well. Tracking individuals erodes trust; listening to public conversation, at the level of themes and tensions, builds it. Somonitor exists to give teams the second without the first, and to make every AI conclusion something a human can check.

About

Why we built Somonitor

Somonitor began with a frustration we kept hearing from marketers: listening dashboards were full of charts nobody trusted. A sentiment line would dip, someone would ask why, and the answer was a shrug or a week of manual reading.

The name is simple. 'So' for social, 'monitor' for the job. The work is less simple: listening continuously, decoding meaning rather than counting words, and making every conclusion checkable against the posts behind it.

We built on SOMIN because its concept engine already did the hard part, tying every concept to its evidence. Somonitor adds the always-on layer that brand, comms and insight teams need day to day.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Somonitor?

Somonitor is an always-on social and brand listening app. It measures share of voice, decodes conversation signals such as tones, emotions and tensions, and raises early warnings when a topic starts to shift. It is built on SOMIN's concept engine, and every insight links to the public posts behind it.

How is Somonitor different from a mention tracker?

A mention tracker counts how often your name appears. Somonitor reads what people mean. It groups conversation into concepts, compares how you and competitors are discussed, and shows the specific posts that support each conclusion, so the output is an argument you can check rather than a number you have to trust.

Does Somonitor track individual people?

No. Somonitor listens to public conversation and reports patterns at the level of themes, concepts and communities. It is designed around listening rather than tracking individuals, because audiences rightly distrust brands that seem to know too much about them.

What role does SOMIN play?

SOMIN, an AI audience-research platform, is Somonitor's technology partner. Its concept engine decodes posts into tones, emotions and tensions and keeps every tag traceable to its evidence. Somonitor adds the always-on monitoring, share-of-voice views and alerting on top.

Can Somonitor warn us about a crisis?

Somonitor watches for unusual combinations of rising volume, shifting emotion and spread into new communities around topics you flag as sensitive. Alerts are short and come with the posts that triggered them, so your team can judge severity quickly instead of reacting to a raw spike.
