# Somonitor brand guidelines (v1.0, October 2026)

**Positioning:** The always-on listening app for teams who want to hear their market early and prove what they heard.
**Tagline:** Always-on listening you can check
**Domain / contact:** somonitor.ai · ask@somonitor.ai
**Technology partner:** SOMIN (somin.ai). Show as "Technology partner SOMIN" with the two-tone SOMIN wordmark (SO teal #2DBF9C, MIN dark/white), never merged into our logo.

## Logo
The mark is a radar sweep that closes into a speech bubble: a reminder that the signal we watch for is always a human conversation.
Files: `brand/logo.svg` (light backgrounds), `brand/logo-dark.svg` (dark backgrounds), `brand/mark.svg` (icon), `favicon.svg`.
Clear space = height of the mark. Minimum 24 px tall. No recolouring, stretching, outlines or effects.

## Typography
- Headlines: Space Grotesk 700, tracking -0.03em, sentence case
- Body: Inter 400/500, 17 px / 1.65
- Labels: Inter 600, 12–13 px, uppercase, 0.2em tracking, accent colour
- Corner radius: 12px

## Personality
- attentive
- calm
- precise
- candid

## Voice
Do:
- Lead with what changed and why it matters
- Point to the evidence behind every claim
- Use plain words for emotions and tensions

Don't:
- Inflate a spike into a crisis
- Claim certainty the posts do not support
- Talk about people as data points to be tracked

## Imagery
Electric cyan signals on deep navy: radar sweeps, particle swarms and fine threads connecting conclusions to their source. Abstract, calm, never stock photography of people at screens. All imagery generated with SOMIN image generation; no text in images, no stock, no identifiable real people.

## Calls to action
Every primary CTA ("Request early access") opens an email to ask@somonitor.ai.
