Brand guidelines · v1.0 · October 2026

Somonitor brand

The always-on listening app for teams who want to hear their market early and prove what they heard.

Logo

The mark is a radar sweep that closes into a speech bubble: a reminder that the signal we watch for is always a human conversation. Download: logo.svg · logo-dark.svg · mark.svg

  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the mark on every side.
  • Minimum size: 24 px tall on screen. Use the mark alone below 120 px wide.
  • Never recolour, stretch, outline or add effects. Use the dark version on #0B1B3A and photography.
  • Pair with the SOMIN partner lockup only as “Technology partner SOMIN”, never merged into the Somonitor logo.

Colour

Accent is for actions, highlights and data. Body text on light surfaces uses #23324D; links use #0E7490 to keep AA contrast.

Section label

Social listening where every signal traces back to real posts

Somonitor watches the conversations around your brand, competitors and category around the clock, then tells you what changed, why it matters and which posts prove it. Built on SOMIN's concept engine.

Personality

  • attentive
  • calm
  • precise
  • candid

Voice: do

  • Lead with what changed and why it matters
  • Point to the evidence behind every claim
  • Use plain words for emotions and tensions

Voice: don't

  • Inflate a spike into a crisis
  • Claim certainty the posts do not support
  • Talk about people as data points to be tracked

Imagery

Electric cyan signals on deep navy: radar sweeps, particle swarms and fine threads connecting conclusions to their source. Abstract, calm, never stock photography of people at screens.

All imagery is generated with SOMIN image generation from art-directed prompts: no stock, no text baked into images, no identifiable real people.
